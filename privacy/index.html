PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Alu Romero Atelier ("the atelier", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect on aluromero.com, why, and how you can exercise your rights, in accordance with Québec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25).

1. Person in charge of personal information

The person in charge of the protection of personal information is Alu Romero, artist and owner of the atelier, in Montréal (Québec). For any question or request: atelier@aluromero.com.

2. Information we collect

When you write to us

If you contact us by email or through the contact form, we receive your name, your email address and the content of your message (for example: type of project, wall size, photos). We use it only to reply to your request, prepare a quote, and create or deliver a work.

Audience measurement (only with your consent)

If you click "Accept" on the cookie banner, we turn on Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager, services of Google LLC. They collect usage data: pages viewed, artworks viewed, clicks on the email address or Instagram, language chosen, device and browser type, approximate city or region, and where the visit came from. We use this data in aggregate to understand how the site is used and to improve it. We do not use it to identify you personally or for advertising.

If you decline or ignore the banner, none of these tools are loaded.

Technical data

Like any website, our hosting provider keeps technical logs (IP address, date, page requested, browser) to keep the server secure and running properly. To prevent abuse of the contact form, a non-reversible fingerprint (hash) of your IP address is kept temporarily on the server.

4. Sharing and transfers outside Québec

We do not sell or rent your personal information. It is shared only with the providers needed to run the site and the atelier: our web host, our email provider and, if you consent, Google. These providers may process data outside Québec, including in the United States. We choose them for their recognized security measures, and they may only use your information for the intended purposes.

5. Retention

Email exchanges are kept as long as needed to handle your request and follow up on a commission, then deleted once they are no longer useful. Detailed Google Analytics data is kept according to our account's retention settings, then deleted automatically.

6. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and access to our accounts (hosting, email, analytics) is limited and protected. If a confidentiality incident presents a risk of serious injury, we will notify the people concerned and the Commission d'accès à l'information, as required by law.

7. Your rights

At any time, you can:

  • ask to access the personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
  • ask for it to be corrected if it is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • withdraw your consent, including for audience measurement;
  • ask that your information stop being disseminated, or that any hyperlink attached to your name be de-indexed, where the law allows;
  • ask for your information to be provided to you in a structured, commonly used technological format.

Write to atelier@aluromero.com. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can file a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

8. Minors

The site is not intended for children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect information about them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date of the last update appears at the top of the page.

10. Accessibility

We want this site to be usable by everyone, including people who browse with a keyboard, a screen reader or zoom. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

Measures in place:

  • descriptive alternative text for the artworks (title and technique);
  • navigation and gallery usable with a keyboard, with a visible focus indicator;
  • checked colour contrast and resizable text;
  • reduced animation when your device asks for it ("reduce motion");
  • page language declared in English, French and Spanish for screen readers.

Known limitations: photos of the artworks cannot convey the tactile relief of the metal. If you would like a more detailed description of a piece, write to us and we'll gladly provide one.

Did you run into a barrier on the site? Write to atelier@aluromero.com with the page and the problem. We will reply within 10 business days and offer another way to get the information if needed.
